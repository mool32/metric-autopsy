# Pre-registration v2 — the simulation track's confirmatory validation of metric-autopsy v0.4

**Status: DRAFT 1 (2026-11-09), for the owner's decisions. Nothing here is frozen, coded or run.**
The owner's decision of 2026-11-09 starts it («Тег запушил, дальше предрегистрация v2»). It turns into
one design the requirements of validation v2 (`validation/plans/v2_requirements.md`: `PROJECT.md`'s
"Requirements for v2", items 1–21, with the owner's decisions recorded there, 2026-10-11 to
2026-10-19) and the "v2 test" of each change of `validation/plans/v0.4_spec.md`. What those leave
open is section 10: ten decisions, each with a recommendation. The sizes are not in this draft:
`oc_v2.py` computes them after the decisions (section 4), and the pilot before the key resizes them.
Every number below is cited from the log or document named beside it; none is new.

**The process, as v1's** (`validation/prereg/v1.md`, its status and section 8): the owner's decisions
on this draft; the code of section 2, each file with its tests, the panel's code never importing the
engine; the checks before the tag by code independent of the engine (sections 3.1 and 3.3); a
rehearsal of the whole run on development backgrounds at the run's settings; independent reviews of
the code by separate agents, posted unedited on the pull request, until one finds no critical defect;
the owner's approval («утверждаю»); an annotated frozen tag, whose push starts the run. From then on
nobody decides anything, and the engine and the protocol change only through a deviations journal.

**The track.** v2 is the simulation track: it measures what the engine does where the truth is
planted. Whether the engine is needed at all is decided on real claims
(`validation/plans/real_claims.md`, the main track; the owner, 2026-10-11), whose controls measure the
same exchange on real data (π\*_real); v2's π\* is compared with it as a check of the simulation.
Scope: scRNA-seq counts only, as v1's (README, Status).

## 1. What the validation tests

The claim under test is about the validator, not about any biology:

> On datasets whose truth is known by construction — the biology, and the metric's validity by a
> mechanism independent of GATE 4's injection —, metric-autopsy v0.4 (frozen), run as a user runs
> it, (a) rarely certifies a false claim on the cards at risk (a valid or ambiguous metric);
> (b) rarely gives a verdict wrong for its reason (a label and cause outside the allowed set);
> (c) rarely calls a valid metric invalid; (d) commits to a correct definite verdict where the design
> makes the truth establishable; and (e) its gates pay off against a careful test over donors up to a
> share of true effects among the claims that the run measures: the threshold π\*.

**(e) is v2's primary outcome** (the owner, 2026-10-11; requirement 18): what the gates add over (b),
a test over donors without gates, and over (c), the engine with GATE 0, 2, 4 and 5 off (section
3.7). (a)–(d) are the criteria (section 6), counted on the cards at risk (requirement 1).

**Scope of inference.** The rates generalize to repeated samples (donors and cells) from the
backgrounds selected by the rule of section 3.1; to the artifacts planted (section 3.5); to the metric
types of section 3.2; and to the planted causes of section 3.3. A metric's validity is measured for
those causes' mechanisms — the latent program's, the mixture's —, not for regulatory coordination at
large (the caveat of circularity, `v2_requirements.md` section 3). The development data — v1's
datasets (v1.md section 6: a new version is validated only with a new pre-registration, a new key
and new datasets), the real pilot's datasets (`real_claims.md`, level A), the dev set
(`validation/probes/`) — play no part in the rates.

## 3. The panel

### 3.1 Backgrounds

**Fresh backgrounds, selected by a rule after the tag** (as v1's, v1.md section 3.1): v1's B1 and B2 and the
real pilot's datasets are development data. The rule, to be fixed in `select_backgrounds_v2.py` with
the owner's decision D2:
- raw counts; donor (or animal) ids that name one individual, checked as below; the chemistry
  recorded, and declared a stratum where it is mixed (requirement 8);
- enough donors for the designs of section 3.5 (v1's: two groups of 8 donors for N1, 24 animals
  split in two for N7; v1.md section 3.2); several cell types, each with enough cells per donor, for
  the conditions of composition (change 7's v2 test);
- **no condition on one background only** (requirement 8): every condition on at least two
  backgrounds, the rates reported per background and pooled;
- **the source shared within every comparison** (requirement 7's check, the TMS lesson): no
  comparison of cells from two generations of data.

**Checks by code independent of the engine** (requirements 13–15), coded and tested before the tag
and run by the selection after it on each candidate in the rule's order, as v1 checked its
extractions (v1.md section 3.1): each donor id one individual of one sex (by Xist and the Y genes);
the strata the engine will form; the source of every comparison; the background run end to end on
its real, sparse data with the run's loader, gene cut and library size (D1: the sparse loader; the
cut that changed CP10k's denominator; change 10's v2 test); each probe checked by arithmetic against
the data's composition (for the module probe the share of cells with the marker times its response
there). A candidate that fails a check is passed over for the rule's next, with the reason listed.
The anchors (T3) are checked before the tag, and each design's smallest attainable p before
registration (requirement 17).

**GATE 6 and the content estimand** (requirement 10): GATE 6 needs a second dataset — two disjoint
halves of a background's donors, or a second background of the same tissue (D3). The content
estimand needs spike-ins (without them the engine calls a content comparison UNIDENTIFIABLE,
`effect.py`) and replicates: a dataset named before the tag, as v1 named its B3 (Buettner et al.
2015, with ERCC; development data now), with at least three independent captures or donors per
group, as v1's content anchor needed for a definite outcome (v1.md section 3.2, R2), checked by the
rehearsal (D3); without one, the content estimand is dropped with its reason.

**Development backgrounds** (requirement 16): the rehearsal before the tag runs the whole pipeline at
the run's settings on B1, B2 and the pilot's datasets, and every comparison with v1 uses v1's
denominators.

### 3.2 Metrics

The types of the real claims, each with a probe v0.4 has (requirement 12; change 3's v2 test asks for
two scales of one construct):

| Type | Metrics | Probe (GATE 4) | SESOI's construct |
|---|---|---|---|
| a pair's coordination | `norm_pearson` and `mi_3bin` of the same pairs | `coupling` of the pair | a change of the correlation of the pair's log expected expression |
| a set's coordination | `set_norm_pearson` (by name since 96a1663); GCL only with D4 | `coupling_set` over the set (`couple="all"`) | a change of the mean correlation over the set's pairs |
| a marker's mean expression | the mean of log1p CP10k of the gene, and of its raw counts | `module` of the gene | a change of its natural-log expression |
| useless | a random number; a constant (as v1's N6a and N6b) | the probe of the claim's type | as the type's |

The pair and set metrics are the engine's reference metrics by name; the marker metrics are the
panel's own functions, passed through the Python API as a user passes a metric (the engine has no
reference metric of a marker's mean). Each pool pair carries a negative control drawn by a rule as
v1's (v1.md section 3.1), which v0.4 reports and does not fail (change 13 as coded, CHANGELOG
[0.4.0.dev0]).

**Two scales of one construct** (change 3's v2 test): `mi_3bin` and `norm_pearson` of the same pairs,
and a marker's mean of log1p CP10k and of raw counts, run at one SESOI of the construct on the same
effects and nulls. Where both are valid by the truth (section 3.3) their verdicts are compared (P8),
and no definite verdict may follow from a SESOI larger than the metric's range between the
replicates.

Metrics of noise and entropy move to the next version (requirement 12, narrowed on 2026-10-11).

### 3.3 The truth about a metric's validity, independent of GATE 4

v1's oracle called a metric valid when it responded to GATE 4's own injection, so its S5 measured
GATE 4's sampling error (requirement 6; Review 1 B7; D1). No real control for coupling has been
verified (`v2_requirements.md` section 3: Marsh et al. 2022 removed; mosaic loss of Y and the
promoter–reporter lines unchecked), so v2 plants its causes, each labelled with its mechanism, and at
least one independent of GATE 4's (D1):

- **T1, a latent program in a generative model fitted to each background:** per gene and cell type a
  count model, and the dependence between genes from a Gaussian copula of the background. A program
  adds a shared latent factor of strength s to the log means of the genes it couples (a pair, a
  set) or a fold change to a marker in a share of the cells. The truth about a metric on a pool item
  is its population response to the program at the dose of the SESOI's construct change, computed by
  simulation from the model — never through GATE 4's thinning.
- **T2, a mixture of real cells of one type in two states,** mixed in donor-specific proportions:
  the coupling, or the marker's share, that the mixture creates is the truth; real cells, real noise.
- **T3, natural causes, only where checked before the tag** (mosaic loss of Y with a DNA-based call;
  the Tabula Muris Senis mice measured by FACS and by droplet, once code independent of the engine
  has shown them shared, within one age or within the later time points, since the 3-month FACS
  mice are by every sign the original Tabula Muris's: `v2_requirements.md` sections 3 and 7):
  single anchors reported one by one, not rates.

The requirements' other candidates are not truth classes here: external biology (pairs of one
protein complex, or co-expressed across many independent datasets) says that a coupling exists, not
its size at the SESOI, so it cannot cut the classes below; physical standards (mixtures of known
proportions, spike-ins) serve the content estimand where D3 finds a dataset.

GATE 4 keeps its thinning injection: v2 measures whether the engine's probe sees what an independent
mechanism makes. **The classes** — valid, blind, ambiguous — are cut on the truth's response in the
metric's units at the SESOI's construct change, with thresholds fixed before the pilot (D5); the
oracle that applies them never imports the engine, as in v1.

### 3.4 Pools: pairs, sets and markers

- **Pairs** (requirements 4 and 11): drawn at random within each expression level, beside the most
  correlated and weakly correlated pairs, each pair's stratum recorded; enough of them that **at least
  200 gene pairs are at risk** (a valid or ambiguous metric) for the primary outcome (the owner,
  2026-10-12; v1 had 18: `v1_correction_rule.log`, section 5), and that every cell's effective size
  after the design effect by pair reaches its size of section 4.
- **Sets:** of the sizes the real claims use, by expression level (change 9's v2 test: in B1 71.1% of
  the 1,500 most expressed genes lie at v1's medium and low levels, `gcl_gene_levels.log`).
- **Markers** (changes 1–2's v2 tests): present in 10–40% of a type's cells; present in all cells of
  one group and absent in the other (as Xist and the Y genes); markers of single counts at depths from
  1,000 to 20,000 counts per cell.

The key draws each dataset's pool item uniformly and independently of its condition (v1.md section 3.2).

### 3.6 Outcomes and their scoring

**Scored by cause** (v1.md section 3.2): every card is one outcome, a (label, cause) pair, from
`report.decide_cause` of v0.4, whose causes are `report.CAUSES`. v1's table of allowed outcomes
carries over by truth class (blind or useless, valid, ambiguous) and data (null; real effect with
\|Δ\*\| below or at least the SESOI), with v0.4's outcomes added:
- **EFFECT BELOW SESOI** (change 14; the owner, 2026-10-17): on null data no false effect; on a real
  effect with \|Δ\*\| ≥ SESOI a false conclusion; with \|Δ\*\| < SESOI correct. (b) gets the same rule.
  The engine gives it only with a declared SESOI (`report.decide_cause`), so the floor's cards (E7)
  cannot; it is a cell of its own on N2, N5, N8 and E1 (change 14's v2 test).
- **INCONCLUSIVE with `detected_only_after_correction`** (change 6), `empty_domain`, `bias_unsized`
  and the other INCONCLUSIVE causes: never definite, allowed everywhere, as in v1.
- **UNIDENTIFIABLE**, definite as in v1: with `unidentifiable` allowed for a content claim without
  spike-ins; with `unmeasured` (fewer replicates with a value than the rule needs) allowed where the
  oracle finds the metric unmeasured on the card's data; elsewhere outside the allowed set.
- **REFUSAL** (GATE 0, `metric_invalid_gate0`): allowed everywhere and never definite, as in v1; its
  rate on valid metrics is measured on N11.
- The dossier's flag `DOSSIER_ONLY` changes nothing: the verdict is scored.

A label that does not match its cause, or a cause the engine does not have, is a rule violation; a
missing report or an exception is a crash (v1's S7a and S7b).

### 3.7 The comparators

Every card is run three times (requirement 18; `v2_requirements.md` section 1):
- **(b) a test over donors without gates:** the donor-level metric values compared by the engine's
  graded rule, coded apart (an exact permutation over donors at four or more a group, the t interval
  at three, no verdict at two; `effect.py`), a TOST at the SESOI for absence, and EFFECT BELOW SESOI
  where it detects an effect in the claimed direction whose 90% interval lies inside ±SESOI, so that
  the comparison measures what the gates add, not a difference in the logic of the verdict. It never
  imports the engine;
- **(c) the engine with GATE 0, 2, 4 and 5 off;**
- **the engine, v0.4**, as a user runs it.
(a), the test over cells, is reported, not compared. The SESOI that (b) uses is the one the engine
would use in the metric's units (section 3.9), so that a difference between them is the gates'.

### 3.8 The key, blinding and the run

As v1's (v1.md section 3.3): a drand round named before it exists, fetched and verified after the tag
(`beacon.py`); the assignment of conditions, pool items and sides by that key; the run in Actions, one
attempt per job, the results committed to their own branch; nothing re-run after the key. The
annotated tag's message carries the approval, its date and the sha256 of every frozen file.

### 3.9 The engine as a user runs it

v0.4's rules, never the oracle's settings (requirement 9): the SESOI on the construct's scale (change
3), GATE 4's probe dosed to it and its response s_m the SESOI in the metric's units; the design's floor
where no SESOI is declared (change 4); `delta_min` recorded and unused. Where the rule leaves a choice
— a declared SESOI below the floor, `--sesoi-scale observed`, the replicate column —, the cards with
the default and the cards with a typical user's choice are separate conditions (`v2_requirements.md`
section 5).

## 4. Sizes, precision and compute

Not in this draft: `oc_v2.py` computes them after section 10's decisions, by these rules.
- **Every criterion is a cell of the cards at risk**, per condition, level and metric type, sized so
  that it fails with probability ≥ 0.95 at an error rate named in advance (D6) and passes a sound
  validator with probability ≥ 0.95, at the design effect expected by donor and by gene pair
  (requirements 1 and 4). The blind and useless cards form cells of their own, so that dilution cannot
  make a criterion easy (v1's S1 cells held 253–271 cards at risk of 790, `v1_null_cells.log`).
- **The sound validator** is one sound within the engine's correction family (requirement 5), so that
  what the criteria predict for an artifact outside the family (N8; N3 against change 6) is explicit.
- **The primary outcome's precision:** at least 200 pairs at risk, so that the 95% interval of π\* over
  pairs is within ±0.1 (the owner, 2026-10-12; `v1_correction_rule.log`, section 5).
- **The pilot before the key** measures the mix of truths on each background and resizes the cells
  (v1's pilot measured the mix and did not resize: `oc_pilot.log`).
- **Each design's smallest attainable p** is checked before registration (requirement 17).
- **Compute:** three analyses per card (section 3.7); a timing pilot sets the shards (v1 split its
  pilot over parallel jobs where one job came near Actions' limit, v1.md section 8); the budget is
  D8's.

## 5. Primary outcomes

- **P0, the threshold π\* = r / (r + l/4 − r₁)** per stratum (truth class, level, metric type), against
  (b) and against (c), with its 95% interval over cards and its design effects by donor and by gene
  pair (`v2_requirements.md` section 1): r and r₁ the false conclusions the gates remove per null and
  per effect card, l the true detections they lose per effect card, one false conclusion worth four
  lost detections (the owner, 2026-10-12). Whether P0 is a measurement only or also a criterion is D7.
- **P1 false SUPPORTED** on the cards at risk, per cell; and on the blind and useless cards apart.
- **P2 outcomes outside the allowed set**, rule violations and crashes apart.
- **P3 correct definite outcomes** on the establishable cards, reported per stratum with the cards at
  risk beside every rate (requirement 19).
- **P4 false "metric invalid"** on the metrics valid by section 3.3's truth, and GATE 4's PASS on
  the blind ones, per level (changes 1 and 3).
- **P5 false NO DETECTABLE EFFECT** where it is not allowed; EFFECT BELOW SESOI as scored in 3.6.
- **P6 the mirrored wording:** the share of mirrored pairs of cards whose verdict and cause coincide.
- **P7 the errors a correction makes** (requirement 20): per artifact condition, the cards whose
  corrected effect is detected while the raw one is not, and those where the correction removed a raw
  false effect; the absences only the corrected data show (E4).
- **P8 two scales of one construct** (change 3): on the cards where both metrics are valid by the
  truth, the share whose verdicts agree; the definite verdicts with a SESOI larger than the metric's
  range between the replicates.
- **P9 the module probe on single-count markers** (change 2): GATE 4's response against the
  composition change's alone, and GATE 4's PASS on blind markers, per depth.

Every rate as k/n with its Clopper-Pearson interval, and its design effects by donor, by pair and by
both (`v1_pair_deff.py`'s estimator), for every cell (requirement 4).

## 6. Success criteria

**Principle** (requirements 1–4): every criterion passes a sound validator with probability ≥ 0.95
and a validator whose error is the rate named in advance (D6) with probability ≤ 0.05, at the design
effect expected; the criteria together pass a sound validator with probability ≥ 0.90 (v1's third
round). Every analysis a statement rests on is pre-registered, the test of each high-level null cell
against its nominal with its correction over the cells; everything else is marked post hoc
(requirement 2).

**The criteria** (each a cell or a family of cells; the thresholds from `oc_v2.py`):
- **S1 false SUPPORTED** on the cards at risk, per null and artifact condition, level and metric type;
  S1b the same on the blind and useless cards.
- **S2 the predictions, each with a cell that can confirm it** (requirement 3): N8's excess (4.3%
  against 2.5%) and N3's at f = 0.4 on the high level (8.4% against 2.5%), each naming every criterion
  it binds.
- **S3 decisiveness:** correct definite outcomes on the establishable cards, per stratum.
- **S4 false "metric invalid"** on valid metrics, by section 3.3's truth.
- **S5 false NO DETECTABLE EFFECT** on blind or useless metrics and on real effects at or above the
  SESOI.
- **S6 the mirrored wording:** no pair of mirrored cards with different verdicts or causes, without
  exception.
- **S7 rule violations:** none (v1's S7a); **crashes:** at a nominal of 0.1% by the principle (v1's
  S7b, the owner's fifth round).

**Failure policy** (v1.md section 6): a criterion that fails is published as it is, with the number
that failed it; no rescue language and no re-run. A new version of the engine is validated only with
a new pre-registration, a new key and new datasets.

## 7. Secondary outcomes

The test over cells (a); the specificity line (change 13: effects planted on one item pass it,
artifacts that move many genes alike fail it, its false refusals per level); GATE 5's negative
control as v0.4 reports it, how often it stands out on valid metrics and on metrics that report an
association between unrelated genes (closure under library-size normalization; a shared technical
factor) — change 13's v2 test was written for the proposal of ten controls with a FAIL, which v0.4
did not build; the correction's line (change 19) against N10's gap; the dose–verdict curves;
per-gate sensitivity; π\* on the blind and useless cards (v1: 0.89, `v1_correction_rule.log` section
4); the set metric by level; GCL if D4 includes it. On v1's cards, an illustration after the results,
π\* against (b) was 0.26 at risk, 0.33 under change 6's rule (`v2_requirements.md` section 1):
reported beside v2's, not a criterion.

## 8. Analysis plan

`score_v2.py` on the run's outputs, nothing else: the outcomes, the cells, the criteria, P0 with its
intervals and design effects, and the limitations where a design effect exceeds 1.5 (v1.md section 5).
The results go to the owner and into the record unedited; deviations, if any, into the journal with
the results they touch.
